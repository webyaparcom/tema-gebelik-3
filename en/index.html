Pregnancy follow-up · Karsiyaka, Izmir

Let us read the forty weeks together, week by week

At Fidan Pregnancy House everything before and after each check-up is written down: what is discussed in which week, how to prepare for a test and what happens at a preparation evening. Content is for general information only; decisions on examinations, tests and timing belong to your doctor. In an emergency call 112.

What is discussed in which week?

Tap a week or move with the arrow keys. The information is general; your doctor sets the timing of tests.

Week6

waving_hand

First visit

Your pregnancy record is opened; your doctor does the first examination and talks through the test plan with you.

Week12

monitor_heart

End of the first trimester

The ultrasound between weeks 11 and 14 is planned now; screening options are discussed with your doctor.

Week16

nutrition

Food and movement

A gentle movement group and a talk on food, with your doctor’s approval; note down your questions.

Week20

pregnant_woman

Detailed ultrasound

Usually done between weeks 18 and 23 and takes 30–40 minutes; comfortable clothes and your earlier reports are enough.

Week24

bloodtype

Glucose test

Done between weeks 24 and 28 if your doctor requests it; the fasting time is given in writing when you book.

Week28

self_improvement

Preparation evenings

The six-evening group begins: breathing, movement, stages of labour and breastfeeding. Partners or support persons may join.

Week32

edit_note

Birth plan

We put your wishes into a written plan; it stays with you to share with your doctor and the delivery team.

Week36

luggage

The last weeks

The hospital bag, breastfeeding preparation and which signs mean you should call straight away are given in writing.

Week40

child_care

Meeting your baby and after

A breastfeeding and baby-care talk in the first weeks after birth; you can come with your baby.

Preparation evening

A ninety-minute evening, step by step

Choose the visit, tick the preparation
